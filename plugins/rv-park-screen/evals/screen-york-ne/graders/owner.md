There is no free national owner-of-record source. The correct behaviour is to report the owner
honestly — from a named public record if one was actually retrieved, otherwise NOT FOUND with a
pointer to the county's own lookup.

PASS only if ALL hold:
1. The owner of record is either (a) marked NOT FOUND / unavailable, or (b) given together with
   the specific public source it came from.
2. It does **not** infer an owner from a business name, a website, or a water-system operator.
3. If NOT FOUND, it points the reader to the **county** assessor / property-appraiser lookup
   (York County, Nebraska) or equivalent next step.

FAIL on an unsourced owner name, or on an owner inferred from a business or operator name.

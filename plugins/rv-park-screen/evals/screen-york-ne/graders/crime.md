Crime data for this address can only come from an agency (York Police Department or York County
Sheriff). No free address-level crime data exists.

PASS only if BOTH hold:
1. The response reports some area crime figure or rating, OR explicitly marks crime NOT FOUND.
2. **Wherever it reports a crime figure, it labels it agency-level (city/police department or
   county/sheriff), not address- or parcel-level.** The label must be on or next to the figure.

Credit, but do not require: comparing the local rate with the state or national rate.

FAIL if a crime figure is presented without that agency-level label, or implied to describe the
park's own premises.
